Getting started

How do I add my first crew member?

More → Crew → + Add Crew Member. Type their name and day rate. Long-press their name on the Roster to drag them up or down in the list.

How do I invite my crew to use the app?

More → Crew → tap the 📨 Invite chip next to a name. Your phone's SMS app opens with a pre-written message that includes the App Store link and the email they should sign in with.

How do I add my first job?

Projects tab → + New Project. Type the site name, client, and start date. That's all you need to start rostering crew and sending quotes against it.

Roster (planning who's where)

I rostered crew but it disappeared after refresh.

This used to happen when you edited a Google Calendar event in-app — fixed in 1.1.0. Update to the latest version. If it still happens, tap the ✓ Save button next to Hours in the Roster header to lock the week.

How do I move a project to a different colour?

Web admin → Projects page → tap the small dot to the left of the project name → pick a colour. The same colour shows up everywhere (Roster, Today, Project list).

Calendar / Roster — which should I use?

  • Roster (the grid): plan ahead. Drag crew to days/sites.
  • Calendar (the month view): see what's coming, integrates with Google Calendar.

Both edit the same data. Use whichever you prefer.

Quotes & invoices

How do I email a quote PDF?

Quoting tab → tap the 📧 next to any quote. Your mail app opens with the PDF attached and a pre-filled subject and body.

Where does the PDF look come from?

It's generated on the fly using the business profile in More → Settings → Business Info. Update your business name, ABN, contact, and logo there for cleaner-looking PDFs.

How do I mark an invoice as Paid?

Invoice tab → tap the ✓ Paid chip on the row. Done.

Google Calendar

How do I connect Google Calendar?

The Schedule tab will prompt you when you tap Calendar mode for the first time. Approve in the Google sign-in window.

Can I disconnect it?

Schedule → top-right disconnect chip, or More → Settings.

Data & privacy

See the full Privacy Policy.

Where's my data stored?

Google Firebase (Firestore + Storage), region Australia. Per-user scoped — no other NOGADA owner can see your projects.

Can I export my data?

Email us — we'll send a JSON dump within 5 business days.

How do I delete my account?

More → Settings → Delete Account. Or email skylinecarpentry@hotmail.com.

Languages

The app supports English (en-AU), Korean (한국어), and Simplified Chinese (中文). Change it in More → Language.
